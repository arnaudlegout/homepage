Research, training and consulting in data science

I am a senior research scientist at Inria, in Sophia Antipolis, France, and I hold a habilitation to supervise research (HDR). For more than twenty years, I have been analyzing data at very large scale to understand the Internet, social networks and privacy.

In parallel, I have been training teams in Python and data science since 2011, and I work with companies as an independent consultant.

Research

My work covers Internet measurement, social networks and information propagation, security and privacy, mobile systems and peer-to-peer content distribution. It is published in the leading conferences of these fields.

Four examples illustrate the approach:

  • the analysis of the entire Twitter social graph, more than five hundred million accounts, which established that most shared articles are never read (ACM SIGMETRICS 2014, ACM SIGMETRICS 2016);
  • the analysis of the whole Bitcoin blockchain, sixteen billion links, to find funds that are lost forever;
  • years of measurements of BitTorrent exchanges, which showed that the anonymity promised by some tools did not exist (LEET 2010, LEET 2011, ACM SIGCOMM/USENIX IMC 2011);
  • the crowdsourced measurement of exposure to radio frequencies with the ElectroSmart app, among 254,410 users in 13 countries from 2017 to 2020 (Environment International).

Several of these works were covered by the international press, including the New York Times, the Washington Post, Fortune, El Diario and Le Monde. My recent work also covers the effect of AI-style suggestions on the accuracy of decisions, the identification of illicit Bitcoin addresses in underground forums, and health discourse on social networks.

I supervised six Ph.D. theses to completion, served on the editorial boards of Proceedings on Privacy Enhancing Technologies (2021–2023, then 2026–2027) and Elsevier Computer Networks (2012–2016), and on the program committees of conferences such as ACM SIGCOMM, ACM IMC, ACM CoNEXT and PETS.

The full list of my publications is in the research section of this site, which also presents my projects, my Ph.D. students and my teaching, as well as on Google Scholar and on my HAL CV.

Teaching

The Python MOOC I designed with Thierry Parmentelat on the FUN platform has gathered more than 150,000 learners since 2014; it is also used by Sorbonne Université and CentraleSupélec.

I teach master's courses at Université Côte d'Azur, today a course on blockchain and privacy in the Ubinet master's program, and previously courses on peer-to-peer applications, scientific communication and an introduction to data science. Since 2024, I have also taught the ComEng course at Eurecom, on oral and written communication for engineers.

Since 2011, I have also trained engineers and researchers in companies: this is the subject of the training section.
